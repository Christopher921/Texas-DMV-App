Privacy Policy

Texas DMV Prep 2026 by ChowChow Labs. Last updated November 1, 2026.

What stays on your iPhone

Your answers, exam history, flags and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run accounts or servers for the app, and this data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We receive no payment card details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on the device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Premium users see no ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. You can turn off tracking at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also collect crash logs and performance data, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use. Google may use this data to diagnose problems, for analytics and for advertising, and performance data may be associated with you. It is not used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

You can delete your study history from this device in Settings.

Your study history is never shared with advertisers. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of its own.

Email

If you email us (for example, to report a question error), we use your message only to respond and to correct content.

Children and teens

The app is a study aid that may be used by teens preparing for a learner's permit. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the app is not directed to them. Ads are served by Google AdMob as described above.
